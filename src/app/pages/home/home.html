About Kerala

Kerala, known as God's Own Country, is a state on the southwestern coast of India renowned for its lush landscapes, backwaters, and rich cultural heritage. The Government of Kerala is committed to delivering citizen-first public services, upholding democratic values, and ensuring the welfare of every individual across the state. Our ministry works to strengthen governance through transparency, accountability, and inclusive development for all communities.

Our Commitment

The Government of Kerala is committed to building an inclusive, equitable, and sustainable society. Through progressive policies in education, healthcare, women's empowerment, and digital infrastructure, we continue the legacy of being a model state for the rest of India. This ministry works every day to ensure that every citizen of Kerala has access to opportunities, justice, and a dignified life.
