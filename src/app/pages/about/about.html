Who We Are

The Ministry of Kerala is the principal governing body of the state, responsible for administering public services, upholding democratic institutions, and ensuring the welfare of all 3.4 crore residents across 14 districts.

Our Mission

To deliver transparent, accountable, and citizen-first governance that promotes inclusive growth, cultural preservation, and sustainable development across Kerala.

Our Values

We are guided by transparency in every decision, integrity in every action, and a deep commitment to inclusivity — ensuring no citizen is left behind regardless of background, region, or circumstance.
